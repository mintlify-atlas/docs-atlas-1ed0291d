The **Recursos Humanos (RRHH)** module is the operational core of MejoraGemeseg for GEMESEG. It covers the entire lifecycle of a guard — from the moment a candidate applies through a Google Drive folder, through their active assignment to a client entity, all the way to their formal exit and folder archival. Alongside guard management, the module handles document compliance checking, HR document generation from `.docx` templates, training records, and an anonymous complaints inbox. All data is multi-tenant: every record is scoped to a `companyId` and access is controlled by the `RRHH` section permission.

<Note>
**Naming note:** The module was renamed from "Personal" to "Recursos Humanos (RRHH)" at the UI and routing level. React Router paths use `/rrhh/*`, and the section permission key is `RRHH`. However, the REST API prefix remains `/personal/...` and internal file names (e.g. `backend/src/modules/personal/`, `personal.service.ts`) are unchanged — this is an intentional contract that avoids unnecessary risk across ~35 call sites.
</Note>

## What the Module Does

RRHH gives the HR team a single place to:

- **Recruit** externally by reading candidate folders from Google Drive and hiring them with one click.
- **Manage active guards** — personal data, entity assignments, and entry/exit movements tracked against external systems (IsyPlus, IESS, SUT, SICOSEP).
- **Check document compliance** for every guard against per-entity requirements, with traffic-light indicators and manual email reminders.
- **Generate HR documents** (contracts, letters) from `.docx` templates stored in Drive, with variables auto-filled from guard records.
- **Track training records** at the group level with document and evidence attachments.
- **Process complaints and suggestions** via an anonymous-capable form and a Kanban management board.
- **Run internal surveys** scoped to users with an account in the platform.

## Section Gating and Permissions

All routes under `/rrhh/*` are wrapped with `SectionRoute section="RRHH"`, which calls `SectionPermissionGuard` on the backend. The relevant guards in the permission model:

- **View access (`canView('RRHH')`)**: read-only access to all sub-modules except the Kanban management and survey management pages.
- **Write access (`canWrite('RRHH')`)**: required to hire candidates, register guard exits, approve/reject documents, generate contracts, and configure verification systems.
- **Admin role only**: actions that are irreversible or touch raw production data — deleting a Drive employee record, configuring Drive folder roots via API, and merging duplicate cédulas (`CedulaMergeModal`). Note that most RRHH users carry the `EMPLOYEE` role with the `RRHH` section permission, **not** `ADMIN`. The cédula-merge button is intentionally invisible to them.

Two routes have **no** `SectionRoute` guard because any authenticated employee should be able to use them:

- `/rrhh/quejas` — any employee can submit a complaint.
- `/rrhh/encuestas` — any employee can answer a survey they have been assigned.

## Google Drive as the Source of Truth

Google Drive is the authoritative record for all document storage in the RRHH module. The service account `drive-sync@agentes-504115.iam.gserviceaccount.com` reads and writes Drive on behalf of the backend. Three folder roots are **fixed in code** (`HARDCODED_DRIVE_FOLDERS` in `backend/src/modules/personal/constants/hardcoded-drive-folders.ts`) and cannot be changed from the UI:

- `RECLUTAMIENTO` — candidate folders, one sub-folder per vacancy.
- `CAPACITACIONES` — training attachments uploaded directly.
- `VENTAS_CONTRATOS` — sales contract PDFs (shared constant, not editable here).

Three roots remain **user-configurable** via the wrench buttons in the relevant pages:

- `CUMPLIMIENTO` — guard folders structured as `Público|Privado/<Provincia - Entidad>/<Apellidos - Nombres>`.
- `GUARDIAS_ARCHIVO` — destination for archived guard folders after a completed exit.
- `PERSONAL_ADMIN` — administrative staff folders structured as `Nombre Apellido - Puesto`.

<Warning>
Drive folder operations (hiring a candidate, archiving a guard) move real folders. Always use clearly labeled test data when running end-to-end tests, and coordinate with the team before touching the shared production Drive.
</Warning>
